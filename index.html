Features

Everything a new tab should do. Nothing more.

New tab

  • Speed dial: add, edit and drag your sites.
  • Weekday clock and search with Chrome's own engine.
  • Six accent colours, wallpapers or your own image.

Focus timer

  • Click the clock for a Focus / Break timer.
  • Your own lengths, sounds and alerts.
  • Keeps running with every tab closed.

Tasks

  • A quick list in the corner of the new tab.
  • Tick, edit and reorder by dragging.
  • Follows you through your Chrome sync.

Tab sleep

  • Unload background tabs from memory in one click.
  • Optional auto-sleep with your own rules.
  • Pinned, playing and grouped tabs can stay awake.

FAQ

Questions and answers.

Is Neon Deck free?

Yes. There is no paid plan, no ads and no account.

Do my tasks and sites sync between computers?

Yes, through Chrome sync, when you are signed in to Chrome with sync on. Without sign-in they stay on this computer.

Which search engine does the search box use?

The one you chose in Chrome's settings. Neon Deck does not change it.

Does it work on my phone?

No. Chrome on Android and iOS does not support extensions.

How do I remove it?

Right-click the Neon Deck icon in the toolbar and choose “Remove from Chrome”. Your normal new tab comes back at once.
